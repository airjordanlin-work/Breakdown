# Breakdown: Product Spec Status: draft v1. Place at `docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md`. Items marked EXISTS are in the repo today. Items marked PROPOSED are not built. ## 1. Goal Build a phone-first, voice-conversational breakdance coach that watches a dancer practice through the phone camera and talks with them the way a good human coach would: short live cues while they move, a debrief when they stop, and open conversation about technique, goals and progress whenever they want. The product must work with hardware everyone already owns (one phone, no purchase). Wearable IMUs are an optional later accessory that improves accuracy, never a requirement. ### Success looks like - A new user opens a link on an iPhone, props the phone up, picks a move, and gets spoken feedback within 10 minutes with no setup beyond camera and mic permission. - The coach feels like a person: it listens, answers questions, remembers last week, and never talks over the dancer. - Every concrete claim the coach makes about the dancer's body or score is traceable to measured data (grounded). It says "I'm not sure" instead of inventing. - Numbers are measured and published: latency, cue grounding rate, frame rate, cost per session. ### Principles 1. Grounded over fluent. The LLM phrases facts, it does not invent them. 2. Phone first. Every feature is judged on whether it works on a mid-range phone. 3. Human feel is a latency and turn-taking problem as much as a wording problem. 4. Privacy by default. Prefer sending landmarks, not video. 5. Measure before claiming. No metric goes in the README or resume without a reproducible script. ### Non-goals (for now) - Competitive judging or scoring against other dancers. - Native iOS/Android apps (web/PWA first). - Multi-person tracking. - Moves beyond the starter catalog until the loop is proven. - Medical or injury-diagnosis claims. ## 2. Audio must be streamed to the client (see M1). ## 3. The conversational coach ### 3.1 Interaction modes 1. **Live cue mode.** While the dancer is moving. Very short (under 8 words), at most one cue per attempt window, never during a freeze or mid-rotation unless safety related. Triggered by the system, not by the user. 2. **Debrief mode.** Right after an attempt ends (motion stops for N ms). The coach gives a 1 to 3 sentence read on what happened, grounded in that attempt's data, and may ask one question ("Did that feel off the left side?"). 3. **Open conversation mode.** The user speaks any time they are not mid-move: "why does my freeze keep collapsing?", "what should I work on this week?", "show me an easier version". The coach answers conversationally, can pull data via tools, and can change the practice plan. ### 3.2 Turn-taking and voice behavior - Default input is **push-to-talk** (hold a button) because breakdance music and floor noise make always-on listening unreliable. Hands-free with voice activity detection is a later option. - **Barge-in:** if the user starts speaking while the coach is talking, TTS stops within 300 ms and the coach yields. - **Never talk over motion.** Spoken output is queued while the dancer is mid-move and delivered at the next pause, unless it is a safety cue. - Spoken replies are short by default (under 25 words for live cues, up to about 55 for a debrief that explains the fix and encourages, under 60 in open conversation) and use spoken language, not written language. - Coach confirms understanding of corrections ("got it, left hip higher") rather than lecturing. - Filler and acknowledgement sounds are allowed ("mm", "nice") only if they are instant and do not block the real answer. ### 3.3 Human-likeness checklist - Natural, varied phrasing; no repeating the same cue twice in a row (repetition metric tracked). - Consistent persona voice and tone (see personas). - References earlier turns and earlier sessions ("last week your toprock was cleaner on the left"). - Asks at most one question at a time. - Admits uncertainty and limits ("my camera can't see your feet in that pose"). - Adapts level: beginner gets simple language, advanced gets terminology. - Latency feels like a person: first audio within about 1.5 s of the user finishing speaking (target, see NFR). ### 3.4 Personas (PROPOSED) Selectable coach personalities (for example "Hype", "Technical", "Calm"). A persona is a system-prompt fragment plus a TTS voice id plus verbosity settings. Personas change style only, never facts. ### 3.5 Memory (PROPOSED) - Short-term: current session transcript and last N attempts. - Long-term: per-user `coach_memory` entries (goals, recurring faults, injuries/limitations the user states, preferences). The coach writes memory through a tool, and the user can view and delete it. ### 3.6 Grounding contract The model must not state a body-position fact, score, or progress claim unless it came from a tool result or the provided `facts` block for that turn. Implementation: - Each turn gets a `facts` JSON block (current move, last attempt scores, joint deviations, freeze events, recent history). - The model can call tools: `get_recent_attempts`, `get_move_info`, `get_progress`, `set_practice_move`, `save_note`. - Output is checked by a grounding heuristic (extends `_grounding_flags` in `benchmark_pipeline.py`); flagged turns are logged and measured. EXISTS for live cues and the debrief: `backend/app/grounding.py` refuses a reply that names an unmeasured body part or side, a number not in the facts, an angle, points or grade, or a past session, and the templated line is spoken instead. Measuring the flag rate is PROPOSED. - Safe fallback: if the model fails or times out, speak a templated cue from the scorer output. ### 3.7 Safety - Never give medical diagnoses. For pain or injury, advise stopping and seeing a professional. - Encourage warm-up and safe space; refuse to coach risky progressions (for example power moves) without prerequisites when the data shows instability. - Do not store audio or video by default. ## 4. Data model Postgres. UUID primary keys. All timestamps `timestamptz`. Row level security so a user only reads their own rows. ```sql users ( id uuid pk, created_at, display_name text null, level text check (level in ('beginner','intermediate','advanced')), persona_id uuid null references coach_personas(id), settings jsonb default '{}' -- voice, verbosity, ptt vs handsfree, units ); coach_personas ( id uuid pk, name text unique, system_prompt text, tts_voice text, verbosity int, created_at ); moves ( id text pk, -- e.g. 'six_step' name text, category text, -- toprock, footwork, freeze, power difficulty int, description text, dtw_threshold real, reference_path text, prerequisites text[] ); move_keyframes ( id uuid pk, move_id text references moves(id), idx int, label text, joint_angles jsonb, -- {left_knee: 92.0, ...} tolerance jsonb ); sessions ( id uuid pk, user_id uuid references users(id), started_at, ended_at null, device jsonb, -- ua, platform, camera res, measured fps source text check (source in ('webcam','phone','imu','fusion')), summary jsonb null ); attempts ( id uuid pk, session_id uuid references sessions(id), move_id text references moves(id), started_at, ended_at, dtw_distance real, dtw_threshold real, passed boolean, overall_score real, landmark_blob_path text null -- off by default (privacy) ); attempt_joint_results ( attempt_id uuid references attempts(id), joint text, deviation_deg real, direction text, primary key (attempt_id, joint) ); freezes ( id uuid pk, attempt_id uuid references attempts(id), start_ms int, duration_ms int, stability real ); coach_turns ( id uuid pk, session_id uuid references sessions(id), attempt_id uuid null references attempts(id), mode text check (mode in ('live','debrief','conversation')), role text check (role in ('user','coach')), text text, facts jsonb, -- exact facts given to the model tool_calls jsonb, -- tools called and results latency jsonb, -- {stt_ms, llm_first_token_ms, tts_first_audio_ms, total_ms} interrupted boolean default false, tokens_in int, tokens_out int, cost_usd numeric(10,6), grounding_flags jsonb null, created_at ); coach_memory ( id uuid pk, user_id uuid references users(id), kind text check (kind in ('goal','fault','limitation','preference','note')), content text, move_id text null, source_turn_id uuid null, created_at, updated_at, deleted_at null ); progress_snapshots ( id uuid pk, user_id uuid references users(id), move_id text references moves(id), day date, attempts int, pass_rate real, mean_score real, worst_joint text null, unique (user_id, move_id, day) ); eval_cue_labels ( id uuid pk, coach_turn_id uuid references coach_turns(id), grounded boolean, labeler text, notes text, created_at ); ``` Notes: - Pose frames and audio are **not persisted** by default. If a user opts in, store landmark blobs in object storage and reference by path. - `facts` and `tool_calls` are stored so any cue can be audited and used for eval. - Indexes: `attempts(session_id, started_at)`, `coach_turns(session_id, created_at)`, `coach_memory(user_id, kind)`. - Retention job deletes `coach_turns` older than the retention window unless exported. ## 8. API and WebSocket protocol Existing REST/WS messages stay as is (EXISTS). New messages (PROPOSED), JSON over WebSocket except audio frames which are binary with a small header. Client to server: - `hello {session_id?, user_id?, device, capabilities}` - `landmarks {t, frame_id, points}` (only if client-side pose) - `ptt_start {turn_id}` / `ptt_stop {turn_id}` - `audio_chunk {turn_id, seq, codec}` (binary) - `text_message {text}` - `interrupt {reason}` (barge-in) - `select_move {move_id}` - `demo_request {}` (EXISTS). The dancer tapped SHOW ME. The server answers with `demo` and a spoken `coach_cue` in `demo` mode, or `demo_unavailable` when no single move is being practiced. A spoken "show me" waits for push-to-talk (FR-11). Server to client: - `frame {..., tracked, moving, score_result}` (EXISTS). `tracked` is true when a body is reliably detected in this frame, `moving` when the dancer is moving (median joint travel over the last 15 buffered frames, see `backend/app/activity.py`). The coach stays silent and nothing is scored unless both are true. Each `score_result.results` entry carries `fix`: `straighten` or `bend`, the direction the joint needs to go. Moves whose meta lists `features` also get whole-rep entries measured against the reference clip (see `backend/app/move_features.py`): `hips` with fix `twist` (hip twist over the rep) and `leg` with fix `reach` (how far a foot gets out from the hips). These only flag doing too little. `score_result` is sent once per rep (one full window of new tracked frames), with `clean` true when no joint is in the Miss tier. `stats` adds the rep view: `reps`, `clean_reps`, `clean_streak`, `best_streak`, `focus_joint` and `focus_fix` (the joint off most often over the last 5 reps). Keyframe joint targets are measured from the move's reference clip unless the meta file sets `target_angle`. The old `total_points` and `grade` fields are still sent but no longer shown. - `coach_cue {cue, turn_id, mode}` (EXISTS). Caption text, sent before the matching `coach_audio_chunk`, so it still shows if TTS fails or times out. `mode` is `live` for a cue while moving, `debrief` for the summary sent when the dancer stops after scored reps, or `demo` for the line said over a demo. - `voice_status {available, reason}` (EXISTS). Sent once when the socket opens. `reason` says why coach audio is off (for example the Kokoro model file is missing); the UI shows it. - `demo {move_id, move_name, speed, loops, seconds, focus, highlight}` (EXISTS). Play the move's reference clip (`GET /moves/{move_id}/preview`) at `speed` for `loops` plays, ringing the `highlight` preview joints. `focus` is the session's measured focus joint, or null when nothing was flagged yet. Live cues stay quiet while it plays. - `demo_unavailable {reason}` (EXISTS). - `coach_audio_skipped {turn_id, reason}` (EXISTS). Sent after a `coach_cue` caption when no audio follows (TTS missing, timed out or failed). - `state {phase, move_id, attempt_id?}` - `stt_partial {turn_id, text}` / `stt_final {turn_id, text}` - `coach_turn_start {turn_id, mode}` - `coach_text_delta {turn_id, text}` - `coach_audio_chunk {turn_id, seq, codec, final}` (binary). EXISTS: one chunk per cue, codec `wav` (16-bit mono). Frame layout: 2-byte big-endian header length, UTF-8 JSON header, then the audio bytes. See `backend/app/audio_frames.py`. - `coach_turn_end {turn_id, interrupted}` - `attempt_result {attempt_id, score, joints, freezes}` - `error {code, message, recoverable}` REST (PROPOSED): `GET /moves`, `GET /sessions/:id`, `GET /progress`, `GET/DELETE /memory`, `DELETE /me/data`. ## 9. Prompt architecture - **System prompt** = persona fragment + safety rules + grounding contract + style rules (spoken language, length limits). - **Per-turn context** = mode, move info, `facts` JSON, last few turns, relevant memory entries. - **Tools** as listed in 3.6, with strict JSON schemas. - Live cue prompt is separate, tiny, and optimized for latency (current Haiku path). - Prompts live in `backend/app/prompts/` as versioned files, and each `coach_turns` row records the prompt version. ## 10. Glossary - **DTW**: dynamic time warping, aligns two motion sequences of different speed. - **Landmark**: one of 33 body points from MediaPipe Pose. - **Attempt**: one continuous try at a move, from motion start to motion stop. - **Barge-in**: user speech interrupting the coach's speech. - **Grounded**: a claim traceable to measured data or a tool result. - **PTT**: push-to-talk. - **IMU**: inertial measurement unit (accelerometer and gyro).
